Tiny program, one job: it writes a two-sentence horror story to /output/entry.txt. A sleep app keeps catching a man whispering the narrator's name at 3:12 in the morning, her brother swears the voice is his from when he stayed over in June, and then the second sentence spoils that comfort completely. Exactly two sentences, fixed text, same file every run, and it checks itself against the 400 character limit before writing.

The line: "My sleep app recorded a man whispering my name at 3:12 three nights running, so I played it to my brother and he went pale and said that was him, sleep-talking, back when he stayed in June. He moved out in June, and last night's recording has two voices whispering back and forth, and one of them is mine."

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```

This writes the entry to /output/entry.txt. Requires Node 22.18 or later (the grader runs Node 22.23.4).
